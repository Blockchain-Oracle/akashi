---
title: Get a stock quote
description: Get demo-grade quotes for up to five US tickers, each ticker checked against the last session's listings first, the latest price from Twelve Data and the last official close cross-checked against Massive.
---

You will call `/v1/stocks` with tickers and read the price, the market clock and the close check. You need a way to
call Akashi (the [mppx CLI](/docs/calling/curl-and-mppx) is the quickest; see [Calling Akashi](/docs/calling)).

<Callout type="warn" title="Demo-grade data">
  Quotes come from free market-data plans whose terms forbid redistribution. Every quote is labelled
  `demo-only; not for redistribution`. Do not use it for trading decisions.
</Callout>

## When to use it

- **"What is AAPL trading at?"** with the session the price belongs to and whether the market is open.
- **Catching a wrong ticker.** A misspelled ticker comes back `not_found` with suggestions, instead of the price of
  some other instrument.

## Request

<TypeTable
  type={{
    symbols: { type: "string[]", description: "1 to 5 US-listed tickers, e.g. AAPL, SPY, BRK.B. BRK-B, BRK/B and BRK B are read as BRK.B; lower case is accepted.", required: true },
  }}
/>

## Send it

```json title="Request body"
{ "symbols": ["AAPL", "SPY", "APPL"] }
```

```bash title="Terminal"
MPPX_PRIVATE_KEY=0x... npx -y mppx@0.11.0 https://test.agent.pocket.network/v1/live-facts/v1/stocks -J '{"symbols":["AAPL","SPY","APPL"]}' --protocol x402
```

<Callout type="warn" title="Not listed on the portal yet">
  Akashi is registered on Pocket Network (Beta) but not yet listed on the agent portal, so this call works once
  `live-facts` is listed. The answer on this page is real, captured from the running service.
</Callout>

## Reading the answer

- **`status`** is per ticker: `ok`, `not_found` (it did not trade on a US exchange in the last session) or
  `unavailable` (no price source answered in time). `summary` counts them.
- **`price`** with **`price_is_close: true`** is the official close of `session_date`; during a session it is the
  latest price and `price_is_close` is `false`. **`price_as_of`** is the time of the last quote.
- **`close_check`** compares the one number both feeds publish independently: the official close of the same
  session. Twelve Data and Massive both say 329.4 for 29 September, so `agreement` is `agree` (within 0.1 %; up to
  1 % is `minor_diff`).
- **`market`** is the NYSE clock at the time of the answer: `state` is `pre_market` here (04:00–09:30 New York
  time), with the next open and the last close. On early-close days `early_close` is `true` and `closes_at` is 13:00.
- **`APPL`** is not a ticker that traded, so it is `not_found`, with up to three **`suggestions`**: tickers one edit
  away or whose company name matches, most traded first, named from the SEC's company list.
- **`provenance.licence`** is `demo-only; not for redistribution` on every quote.

## How a quote is built

<Steps>
  <Step>
    **Check the ticker.** Massive's grouped daily file lists every US ticker that traded in the last completed
    session with its official close (about 12,600 tickers, ETFs included) and is fetched once per session. A ticker
    not in it is `not_found`, and no quote credit is spent on it.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    **Quote it.** Listed tickers are quoted by Twelve Data in one batch call, pinned to the US listing (SHOP and RY
    also trade in Toronto). Quotes are cached for 60 seconds while the market is open, and after the close has
    settled (30 minutes) until the next open.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    **Cross-check the close.** Twelve Data's close for the session Massive published (or its `previous_close`, once
    Twelve Data has moved on to the next session) is compared with Massive's.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    **State the market.** The NYSE clock comes from the exchange calendar in `python-holidays`: full closures and
    early closes, such as 1 p.m. on the day after Thanksgiving and on Christmas Eve 2026.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Edge cases

<Accordions>
  <Accordion title="Twelve Data is out of free credits">
    The free plan allows 8 tickers a minute and 800 a day. When they run out, a listed ticker still gets the official
    close of the last session from Massive, with `price_is_close: true`, a note saying no live quote was available,
    `twelvedata` in `unavailable`, and the envelope `partial`.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A ticker resolves to an old instrument">
    If a quote's session is older than the last completed session, `freshness` is `stale` and a note says when it
    last traded: halted, delisted, or not the instrument you meant.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="No trade yet today">
    Just after the open, a thinly traded ticker may not have printed yet: its price is the last close and `freshness`
    is `lagging`.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Stock quotes are switched off">
    A deployment without the market-data keys answers `422 unsupported`: "Stock quotes are switched off on this
    deployment."
  </Accordion>
</Accordions>

## Endpoints

| Endpoint | Answers |
|---|---|
| `POST /v1/time` | the time in a zone, city or country, and its next daylight-saving change |
| `POST /v1/holidays` | public and exchange holidays, from three sources compared |
| `POST /v1/business-days` | business days between dates, or a date so many business days away |
| `POST /v1/fx` | exchange rates from the issuing central banks, with each fixing's age |
| `POST /v1/weather` | the current forecast hour from MET Norway, cross-checked by NWS in the US |
| `POST /v1/fact` | a current Wikidata statement (the preferred, still-valid value) |
| `POST /v1/news` | recent headlines from GDELT and Hacker News |
| `POST /v1/stocks` | US quotes: the ticker checked, the last close compared across two feeds (demo-grade) |
| `POST /v1/jobs` | postings from public company job boards |

## Provenance

Every result carries `provenance`:

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `sources` | which sources the value came from |
| `as_of`, `age_seconds` | when the underlying data was true or published, and how long ago |
| `freshness` | `fresh`, `lagging` (on the source's own schedule but behind the others), `stale` or `unknown` |
| `agreement` | `agree`, `minor_diff`, `conflict`, or `single_source` when only one source covers it |
| `spread_pct` | how far apart the sources are |
| `licence`, `attribution` | the terms the data comes with |

A disagreement is reported, not hidden: when the Gambian central bank's naira rate sits 9% from the others, the
answer says `conflict` and lists every fixing.

## Stocks are demo-grade

`/v1/stocks` uses free market-data plans whose terms forbid redistribution, so every quote is labelled
`demo-only; not for redistribution`. A ticker is first checked against the symbols that traded on a US exchange in
the last session; a typo comes back `not_found` with suggestions instead of an unrelated instrument's price.

---
title: Check a claim against its source
description: Ask /v1/claim whether a cited paper, or text you provide, supports a claim, and read the sentence that decides it.
---

You will check whether a source supports a claim attributed to it, and find the sentence the answer rests on. You
need a way to call Akashi (see [Call Akashi](/docs/calling)).

## When to use it

A citation can be real and still not say what it is cited for. Use this when generated text attributes a finding
to a source: "the Lancet paper showed…", "according to Vaswani et al.…".

## How the check works

<Steps>
<Step>

### Find the evidence

If you send `evidence_text`, that text is the evidence. Otherwise the citation is verified first (as in
[Verify citations](/docs/services/cite/verdicts)), and if it is `verified`, `mismatch` or `retracted` and has a DOI, its
abstract is fetched from the first source that has one: Europe PMC, then OpenAlex, then DataCite for arXiv papers.

</Step>
<Step>

### Pick the candidate sentences

The evidence is split into sentences. The six closest to the claim, plus the two best sentences joined with the
sentence that follows each, are scored. The whole abstract is never scored at once.

</Step>
<Step>

### Score them with a natural-language-inference model

Each sentence gets three probabilities: it entails the claim, is neutral, or contradicts it. Only a sentence that
shares a content word with the claim may decide the verdict, so an unrelated sentence cannot "contradict" it.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Request

<TypeTable
  type={{
    claim: { description: "The claim to test, up to 1,000 characters.", type: "string", required: true },
    citation: {
      description: "The source the claim is attributed to: a string or a structured citation. Give this, evidence_text, or both.",
      type: "string | StructuredCitation",
    },
    evidence_text: {
      description: "The source text itself, up to 20,000 characters. When present it is used instead of fetching an abstract.",
      type: "string",
    },
  }}
/>

## Send it

```bash title="Does 'Attention Is All You Need' say Transformers rely on recurrence?"
MPPX_PRIVATE_KEY=0x... npx -y mppx@0.11.0 https://test.agent.pocket.network/v1/citation-verify/v1/claim -J '{"claim": "Transformers rely entirely on recurrence", "citation": "arXiv:1706.03762"}' --protocol x402
```

<Callout type="warn" title="Not on the portal yet">
  Akashi's services are registered on Pocket Network (Beta) but not yet listed on the agent portal, so this call works
  once they are listed. The answers on this page were captured from the running service.
</Callout>

The command pays with a Base Sepolia wallet through x402. To set up a client in code, see [Call Akashi](/docs/calling).

## Reading the answer

- **`verdict`** is one of:

  | Verdict | When |
  |---|---|
  | `supported` | A relevant sentence entails the claim (entailment ≥ 0.80) and does not also look contradicting (contradiction < 0.20) |
  | `contradicted` | A relevant sentence contradicts the claim (contradiction ≥ 0.80) |
  | `insufficient_evidence` | The evidence was read and says neither, or there was no evidence to read (`reasons` says which) |
  | `unverifiable` | The model could not be reached; `retryable: true` |

- **`evidence_sentence`** is the sentence (or sentence pair) that decided the verdict. Quote it.
- **`scores`** are that sentence's entailment, neutral and contradiction probabilities.
- **`evidence_scope`** is `abstract` or `provided_text`, and **`premise_source`** says where the text came from
  (`europepmc`, `openalex`, `datacite` or `provided_text`).
- **`model`** names the NLI model that scored it.
- **`citation`** is the full verification of the cited work, the same object `/v1/verify` returns.

## Edge cases

<Accordions type="single">
  <Accordion title="The first claim after a quiet spell comes back unverifiable">
    The model loads on demand and unloads after 15 idle minutes. A claim that arrives while it is loading can come
    back `unverifiable` with the reason "the NLI model is unavailable" and `retryable: true`. Ask again a few
    seconds later.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The citation could not be verified">
    If the cited work is `not_found`, `ambiguous` or `unverifiable`, there is nothing to read: the verdict is
    `insufficient_evidence` with the reason "the citation could not be verified". Send `evidence_text` instead.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="No DOI, or no abstract">
    Abstracts are fetched by DOI. A work without one, or whose abstract no source holds, gives
    `insufficient_evidence` with a reason saying which. Send the text as `evidence_text`.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The claim is about the paper's body, not its abstract">
    Only the abstract is read, so a claim about a result deep in the paper may be `insufficient_evidence`. Send the
    relevant passage as `evidence_text`.
  </Accordion>
</Accordions>

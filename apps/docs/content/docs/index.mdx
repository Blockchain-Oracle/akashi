AI assistants answer confidently, even when they are wrong. They cite court cases that never existed, suggest
software that was never published, and quote prices from last year.

**Akashi checks these things against the original records before the AI answers.** It says what it found, where
it looked, and how current it is.

<ClaimCheck />

## Why it matters

- In 2023, lawyers in New York were fined for filing a brief with court cases that ChatGPT had invented.
- AI coding tools suggest packages that do not exist, and attackers publish malware under those exact names.
- An AI only knows the world up to its training date. Rates, prices and holidays change every day.

## Where Pocket Network fits

Akashi runs on **Pocket Network**, a network where AI agents pay for services one call at a time: no account, no
subscription. Pocket delivers the question and takes the payment. Akashi does the checking.

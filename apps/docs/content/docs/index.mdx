Agents are fluent and often wrong in the same three places: a citation that does not exist, a package or method
that was never published, and a fact that was true last year. Akashi (証, *proof*) checks each of them against the
records themselves and answers with evidence, not an opinion.

<ServiceLedger />

## What an answer looks like

In *Mata v. Avianca* (S.D.N.Y. 2023), a brief cited *Varghese v. China Southern Airlines Co.*, 925 F.3d 1339
(11th Cir. 2019), a case a chatbot had invented. This is Akashi checking that citation: a real response from the
running service.

The verdict comes with its reason. Page 1339 of volume 925 of the Federal Reporter lies inside another case,
*J.D. v. Azar* (pages 1291–1349), so no case can begin there. The source is named (the Caselaw Access Project, CC0),
the link lets anyone check it, and `retryable: false` says asking again will not change the answer.

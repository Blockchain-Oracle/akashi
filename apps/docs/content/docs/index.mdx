Agents are fluent and often wrong in three places: a citation that does not exist, a package or method that was
never published, and a fact that was true last year. Akashi checks each of them against the records themselves.

## What an answer looks like

In *Mata v. Avianca* (S.D.N.Y. 2023), a brief cited *Varghese v. China Southern Airlines Co.*, 925 F.3d 1339
(11th Cir. 2019), a case a chatbot had invented. This is Akashi checking that citation, a real response from the
running service:

The verdict comes with its reason: page 1339 of volume 925 lies inside another case, *J.D. v. Azar* (pages
1291–1349), so no case can begin there. The source is named, the link lets anyone check it, and
`retryable: false` says that asking again will not change the answer.

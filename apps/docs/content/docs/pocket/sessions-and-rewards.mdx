---
title: Sessions, claims and rewards
description: How a relay to Akashi becomes settled POKT.
---

<SessionTimeline />

- **Price:** 40,000 CU per relay for `citation-verify`, 20,000 for `code-reality-check` and 10,000 for `live-facts`
  (1 CU = 0.04 uPOKT on Beta). Heavier checks cost more.
- **Claim:** after each session the RelayMiner submits one claim per application with the total compute units.
- **Proof:** on Beta almost every claim needs a Merkle proof; a missing proof forfeits the session.
- **Why Akashi never returns a 5xx:** a relay answered with a 5xx loses its reward. An upstream outage becomes a
  `partial` answer instead.
